Our Mission

Our mission is to harness the power of machine learning to improve healthcare and empower individuals with tools to take charge of their heart health. We aim to create accessible, accurate, and user-friendly applications that help predict heart disease risk and support informed healthcare decisions.

Project Overview

The Heart Disease Prediction application predicts the likelihood of heart disease using health-related metrics. Built using Machine Learning and a Flask interface, users can enter information such as age, cholesterol levels, and activity metrics to receive prediction results.

The project continuously evolves with features such as authentication, downloadable reports, and enhanced prediction capabilities.
